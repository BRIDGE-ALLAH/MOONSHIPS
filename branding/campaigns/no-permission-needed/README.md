# NO PERMISSION NEEDED
## MOONSHIPS campaign kit · v1.0

**Campaign:** No Permission Needed.  
**Master identity:** Your Infrastructure. Your Rules.  
**Movement line:** Build Beyond the Default.  
**Creative direction:** Space Punk / Underground Future  
**Primary audience:** independent developers, indie makers, small product teams, and infrastructure operators.

---

## Campaign idea

Builders should be able to choose where their software runs and retain a clear view of the infrastructure beneath it. MOONSHIPS makes that choice practical through an open-source, self-hosted control plane for applications, containers, databases, and servers.

"No Permission Needed" is a statement about agency and infrastructure choice. It is not a claim that security, law, provider terms, or operational safeguards can be ignored.

## Message hierarchy

1. **Hook:** No Permission Needed.
2. **Promise:** Your Infrastructure. Your Rules.
3. **Explanation:** An open-source, self-hosted control plane for applications, containers, databases, and servers.
4. **Proof:** Show real product capabilities—deployments, container operations, server connections, databases, backups, history, and rollback—only where implemented and available.
5. **Action:** Explore the repository, read the docs, or try MOONSHIPS.

Never publish the hook without a nearby product explanation or a clear product visual.

## Creative system

- **Canvas:** near-black void (#090B10), with warm chalk (#FFFDF7) for primary text.
- **Signal:** acid green (#C7F464) for key words and action; ultraviolet (#9B8CFF) for a rare counterpoint; orbit cyan (#69D8F2) for technical details.
- **Texture:** restrained photocopy grain, distressed ink, rough paper edges, analog scan noise. Texture must not reduce text contrast.
- **Motifs:** orbital rings, moon/planet forms, star maps, flight markings, signal paths, hand-drawn annotations.
- **Typography:** condensed display lettering for short headlines; system sans for body; monospace for technical labels. Do not rely on an unbundled font for essential content.
- **Composition:** asymmetry, large type, strong negative space, one focal point, one clear CTA.
- **Product truth:** use actual MOONSHIPS UI screenshots for feature proof. Do not fabricate dashboard screens, metrics, customer logos, or testimonials.

## Carousel storyboard (5 slides)

2. **Your stack. Your call.** — Explain infrastructure choice without attacking providers.
3. **Deploy. Operate. Connect. Protect.** — Four capability pillars; use only features present in the current release.
4. **Open-source. Self-hosted. In your control.** — Explain the product category and what self-hosting actually means.
5. **YOUR INFRASTRUCTURE. YOUR RULES.** — CTA to GitHub/docs; do not imply one-click setup unless the current install flow supports it.

## Publishing rules

- One post = one idea, one proof point, one CTA.
- Lead with the human outcome, then explain the product.
- Pair every capability claim with a shipped feature or mark it clearly as roadmap.
- Do not use "unlimited", "zero maintenance", "100% secure", "unbreakable", or guaranteed savings claims.
- Do not imply that self-hosting removes patching, backups, monitoring, or security responsibilities.
- Never put campaign slang into security warnings, destructive confirmations, or migration instructions.
- Avoid forced slang, competitor insults, fake urgency, and fabricated customer proof.
- Use the canonical product name MOONSHIPS. Historical runtime names belong only in technical compatibility contexts.

## Accessibility and QA

- Maintain WCAG AA contrast for body text.
- Do not encode meaning with color alone.
- Keep essential copy as live text in source SVG or platform caption.
- Provide alt text describing the visual and its message.
- Check crop safety at actual platform dimensions.
- Review links, spelling, product claims, and release-specific capabilities before publishing.
